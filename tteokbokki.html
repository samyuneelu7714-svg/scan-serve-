🌶️ Korean Rabokki

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g Korean rice cakes (Tteok)
  • 1 packet instant ramen noodles
  • 4 dumplings (Mandu)
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tbsp Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
  • 1 tbsp Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Sugar
  • 2 Garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 Spring onions (chopped)
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Boil 2 cups of water in a pan.
  2. Add Gochujang, Gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar and garlic.
  3. Mix until the sauce becomes smooth.
  4. Add the rice cakes and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the ramen noodles and cook for another 3–4 minutes.
  6. Add the dumplings and cook until they are fully heated.
  7. Place the boiled eggs on top.
  8. Sprinkle sesame seeds and chopped spring onions.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy!

🍽️ Serving Suggestion

Serve hot with kimchi and a cold drink. This spicy Korean comfort food is perfect for sharing with family and friends.
