🥤 Oreo Milkshake

Ingredients

  • 6 Oreo biscuits
  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • Chocolate syrup (optional)

Instructions

1. Break the Oreo biscuits into small pieces.
2. Add Oreos, milk, ice cream and sugar into a blender.
3. Blend until smooth and creamy.
4. Pour into a glass.
5. Drizzle chocolate syrup on top if desired.
6. Serve chilled and enjoy!
