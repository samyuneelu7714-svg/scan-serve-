🍌 Banana Milkshake

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Instructions

1. Peel and slice the banana.
2. Add the banana, milk, sugar, and ice cream to a blender.
3. Blend until smooth and creamy.
4. Pour into a glass.
5. Serve chilled and enjoy!
