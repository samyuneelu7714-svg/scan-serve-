✨ Aglio e Olio

🛒 Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 5 garlic cloves (thinly sliced)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Boil the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Add sliced garlic and cook until light golden.
  4. Add chili flakes and stir for a few seconds.
  5. Add the cooked spaghetti and toss well.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Add chopped parsley.
  8. Serve hot with Parmesan cheese if desired.
